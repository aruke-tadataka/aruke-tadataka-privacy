# Privacy Policy (Aruke! Tadataka)

Last updated: 2026-11-15

[日本語](../)

"Aruke! Tadataka" (the "App") respects your privacy.
This policy explains what information the App handles.

## 1. Our approach: we do not collect personal information
The App **does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location**.

- The App has no servers of its own.
- There is no account registration or sign-in.
- The App communicates outside your device only to show ads (Section 3), load photos of famous places (Section 4), and display maps (Section 5).

## 2. Information kept on your device
To save your progress, the App stores the following information only on your device. It is never sent off the device.

- **Distance reached**: the farthest point you have reached.
- **Places log**: the famous places whose descriptions you have seen.
- **Quizzes**: the quizzes you have answered correctly.
- **Local dishes**: the local dishes you have collected.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To serve ads, measure impressions, and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as:

- **Device information**: model, OS version, language, etc.
- **Network information**: IP address and the approximate region inferred from it.
- **Ad activity**: that an ad was shown or tapped.

The App does not request tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). The advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used without your permission.
For how Google handles information, see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 4. Photos of famous places (Wikipedia)
To show photos with place descriptions, the App loads article summaries and images from Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation). As with ordinary web browsing, network information such as your IP address is sent to the Wikimedia Foundation.
See the [Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy) for details.

## 5. Maps (Apple Maps)
The map in "Journey Log" uses Apple's MapKit. Apple handles the network communication needed to display the map.
See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) for details.

## 6. Sharing with third parties
We never sell your information. Apart from using the services in Sections 3 to 5, we do not share it with third parties.

## 7. Deleting data
Deleting the App from your device erases all information the App stored on it.

## 8. Children
The App is not primarily directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. Changes to this policy
We may revise this policy as needed. Significant changes will be announced in an app update or on this page.
