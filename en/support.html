FAQ

How do I play?

Tadataka walks automatically. When a gap in the coast, a step, a seagull, or a barrel comes up, tap the screen to jump.

When do I lose health (hearts)?

You lose one heart when you bump into an official, barrel, seagull, wave, falling rock, lightning, or icicle, hit the side of a step, or step into a hot spring pool. Falling into the sea or a hole takes all your hearts. Eating a local dish or local fish, answering a quiz correctly, or clearing a stage restores one heart (a wrong quiz answer costs nothing).

What happens when I run out of health?

You start again with full health from the last point you passed (marked by a tea-house flag). If you fail several times in a row from the same tea house, you can restart with a disciple's help (no damage from bumps for a while). Each stage also has a practice mode where you don't lose health.

Photos of famous places do not appear

Photos are loaded from Wikipedia (Wikimedia Commons), so an internet connection is required. Without one, or if a photo's free license can't be confirmed, a pixel-art scene is shown instead. The author and license are shown under each photo, with a link to the original image page.

When are ads shown?

After every two stages you clear, a full-screen ad may appear before the next stage. No ads are shown in the first three minutes after opening the app.

Are the route and distances historically accurate?

The departure date (June 11, 1800, from Tomioka Hachimangu Shrine in Fukagawa) is historical. The route is an imagined trip around Japan that links Ino Tadataka's ten surveys, and distances are straight lines between about 80 coastal points.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play without registering or signing in. Your progress is stored only on your device and is erased when you delete the App.
